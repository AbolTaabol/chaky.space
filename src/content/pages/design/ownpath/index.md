## Overview
:::

:::column{width="0.8125"}
Breaking into product design often requires access to mentorship, industry projects, and professional networks that remain out of reach for self-taught designers and career switchers. OwnPath is an India-based platform offering an intensive Product Design Fellowship that combines masterclasses from industry experts, personalized mentorship, and real-world projects with companies like Postman and Gojek. The program is built around accessibility, offering scholarships and a cohort model designed to help designers from diverse backgrounds land roles at leading startups and enterprises.

## My role
:::

:::column{width="0.8125"}
As Designer in Residence, I led a complete redesign of the Product Design Fellowship program. I restructured the cohort model, developed curriculum, and built frameworks for community engagement and mentorship. I taught UI Design modules and worked closely with the founding team to optimize operational costs while expanding reach. The goal was to create a sustainable model that could scale internationally without compromising on quality or accessibility.
